❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about CropCare AI.

🌿 How accurate is the disease detection model? +
Our MobileNetV2-based model achieves approximately 90–95% accuracy on the PlantVillage test set. However, real-world accuracy may vary depending on image quality, lighting conditions, and whether the disease is in the 38 supported classes. For best results, take a close-up photo of a single leaf in natural daylight.
🔒 Is my uploaded image data stored permanently? +
Uploaded images are saved locally to the static/uploads/ directory on the server for the purpose of displaying prediction history within your session. No images are shared with third parties or uploaded to any cloud storage. You can clear your history at any time from the History page, which removes all stored references.
🚀 Can I use CropCare AI for my own research or project? +
Absolutely! CropCare AI is an open-source educational project. You are welcome to fork the repository, adapt the model, and build upon the codebase for your own research or applications under the project's open-source license. We only ask that you credit the original project and the PlantVillage dataset authors in your work. If you do build something cool, let us know — we'd love to feature it!
